How Hot How Long

TypeScript · Docker · AWSjkkk HowHotHowLong

HowHotHowLong started as an Alexa skill my wife asked for. She cooks by feel and doesn't need full recipes, but she could never remember the oven setting or how long something should go. She wanted a quick way to ask "how hot, how long" for the things she makes and get just that answer.

It has since grown into a website. You can look up safe internal temperatures for different cuts, find cook times by weight, thickness and method, and plan meals for the week. It's still built around the same idea: the few numbers you need, without wading through a recipe.
